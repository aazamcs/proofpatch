# Our development record

## IBM Bob in our original build
We used IBM Bob during the original development of ProofPatch and consumed our allocated credits. Our original archive, proofpatch_final.zip, contained a reusable Bob skill, regression and acceptance tests, three sample implementations, and a Streamlit interface. Our original baseline, fixture and original regression/acceptance files are retained byte-for-byte in this release.

The archive supplied for release preparation did not include exported Bob sessions or committed development history. Our original source comments describe Bob assistance; the corresponding original task/session reports remain separate evidence to attach. We do not claim those reports are already included.

## Continued local engineering
After using our Bob allocation, we continued improving the project locally with OpenAI Codex assistance. This work covered the verification runner, web interface, exact-snapshot evidence packaging, seven additional acceptance cases, verifier/interface tests, candidate termination and large-export fixes, dependency pins, documentation, and submission materials. These later contributions are not attributed to Bob.

## Reproducibility and scope
Our frozen manifest records the reviewed release reference created during local hardening. It detects differences from that reference; it does not establish original test-before-repair chronology. Hashes identify content, not authorship or time.

Our pagination example uses synthetic data. Our intentionally wrong repair is a negative control. Verification reports come from actual local pytest execution. The app executes the independent verification stage of our workflow without requiring model credits. Its result is scoped to the bundled case and test suite, with human review still required.
